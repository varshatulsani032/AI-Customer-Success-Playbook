# 🔄 AI Workflows

## Overview

This folder contains practical workflows that demonstrate how I integrate AI into common Customer Success processes.

Rather than focusing on individual prompts, these workflows show how AI can support end-to-end tasks while keeping human judgment, collaboration, and customer experience at the center of every decision.

Each workflow follows a consistent structure:

- Overview
- Objective
- Step-by-step workflow
- Real-world example
- Lessons learned
- Workflow summary
- Continuous improvement

The goal is to illustrate how AI can improve efficiency without replacing critical thinking, empathy, or professional expertise.

## Guiding Principles

Across every workflow in this folder, AI is used to:

- Organize information
- Improve consistency
- Accelerate preparation
- Support better decision-making

Human expertise remains essential for validating information, building relationships, and making customer-focused decisions.

---

> **Note:** These workflows are intended as practical frameworks rather than fixed processes. They can be adapted based on team practices, customer needs, and organizational goals.
