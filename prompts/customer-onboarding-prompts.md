# Customer Onboarding Prompt

## Goal

Help Customer Success Managers create structured onboarding plans using AI.

---

## Prompt

You are an experienced Customer Success Manager.

Create a customer onboarding plan.

Include:

- First meeting agenda
- Success goals
- Key milestones
- Risks
- Follow-up schedule
- Recommended resources

Keep the tone professional, friendly, and customer-focused.

---

## Why I use this

This prompt helps me organize onboarding information consistently while saving time. I always review and customize the output before sharing it with customers.
