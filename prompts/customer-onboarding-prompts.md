# 🚀 Customer Onboarding with AI

## Overview

Great onboarding sets the foundation for long-term customer success.

This prompt helps create a structured first draft of an onboarding plan, allowing Customer Success Managers to spend more time understanding customer goals and less time formatting documents.

> **Note:** AI should support the onboarding process, not replace customer conversations or human judgment.

---

# 🎯 Use Case

Use this prompt when:

- A new customer signs up
- Preparing for a kickoff meeting
- Creating an implementation plan
- Building a customer success roadmap

---

# 🤖 AI Prompt

```
You are an experienced Customer Success Manager.

Create a structured onboarding plan for a new customer.

Include:

• Welcome message

• Customer objectives

• Success milestones

• Product setup checklist

• Training schedule

• Potential implementation risks

• Suggested follow-up cadence

• Success metrics

Write in a friendly, professional, and customer-centric tone.
```

---

# 💡 Why I Use This

Rather than starting with a blank page, I use AI to organize onboarding information into a clear structure.

This saves time while ensuring every customer receives a consistent experience.

The final onboarding plan is always reviewed and customized based on the customer's goals, product usage, and business context.

---

# ✅ Best Practices

Always:

- Verify customer-specific information
- Customize milestones
- Remove generic recommendations
- Add internal resources
- Review every AI-generated response before sharing

---
