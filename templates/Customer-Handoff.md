# 🤝 Customer Handoff Template

## Overview

A customer handoff ensures that important information is transferred accurately between teams during key stages of the customer journey.

Whether transitioning from Sales to Customer Success, Implementation to Support, or between Customer Success Managers, a structured handoff helps maintain continuity, reduce knowledge gaps, and create a seamless customer experience.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

Use this template to:

- Ensure a smooth customer transition
- Preserve important customer context
- Improve cross-functional collaboration
- Reduce repeated customer conversations
- Support consistent customer experiences

# 🛠️ How to Customize

Adjust this template based on:

- Customer lifecycle stage
- Internal team structure
- Product complexity
- Customer size
- Organizational processes

The objective is to capture the information that enables the next team to continue the customer journey with confidence.

# 🌍 Example

**Customer:** Acme Solutions

**Business Goal:**
