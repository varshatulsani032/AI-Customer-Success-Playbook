# 📂 Customer Success Templates

## Overview

This folder contains practical templates designed to support common Customer Success activities throughout the customer lifecycle.

These templates provide a structured starting point for planning, documentation, communication, and customer relationship management. They are intentionally adaptable and can be customized to fit different teams, products, and organizational processes.

Where appropriate, AI can assist with drafting and organizing information, but every template is intended to be reviewed and tailored by the user.

---

# 📑 Contents

## 📋 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Template

A structured framework for documenting repeatable processes to improve consistency, knowledge sharing, and operational efficiency.

**Use Cases**

- Internal processes
- Customer support procedures
- Team documentation
- Process standardization

---

## 🎯 Customer Success Plan

A collaborative planning document used to align customer goals, success metrics, milestones, and responsibilities throughout the customer journey.

**Use Cases**

- New customer onboarding
- Account planning
- Success planning
- Implementation tracking

---

## 🤝 Customer Handoff

A template for transferring customer context between teams while maintaining continuity and reducing knowledge gaps.

**Use Cases**

- Sales to Customer Success
- Implementation to Support
- Customer Success Manager transitions
- Cross-functional collaboration

---

## 💚 Customer Health Check

A structured review template for evaluating customer engagement, identifying risks, measuring progress, and planning next steps.

**Use Cases**

- Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)
- Success reviews
- Adoption assessments
- Customer retention planning

---

# 🤖 Using AI with These Templates

AI can help:

- Organize information
- Draft initial content
- Improve readability
- Suggest missing sections
- Summarize customer notes

Human review remains essential to ensure every document reflects the customer's unique goals, business context, and organizational requirements.

---

# 📌 Guiding Principles

These templates are built around a few core principles:

- Keep documentation clear and actionable.
- Focus on customer outcomes rather than activities.
- Standardize processes while allowing flexibility.
- Use AI to improve efficiency—not replace human judgment.

---

> **Note:** These templates are intended as adaptable frameworks rather than fixed documents. Modify them to meet the needs of your team, customers, and organization.
