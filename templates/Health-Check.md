# 💚 Customer Health Check Template

## Overview

A Customer Health Check provides a structured way to evaluate customer success, identify opportunities, address potential risks, and strengthen long-term relationships.

Regular health checks help Customer Success teams understand how customers are progressing toward their goals while ensuring proactive support and continuous value delivery.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

Use this template to:

- Assess customer health and engagement
- Review progress toward business goals
- Identify risks before they escalate
- Discover opportunities for growth
- Plan meaningful next steps

# 🛠️ How to Customize

Customize this template based on:

- Customer maturity
- Account size
- Product complexity
- Review frequency
- Customer objectives

Not every section needs to be equally detailed. Focus on the information that best supports meaningful customer conversations.

---

# 🌍 Example

**Customer Goal**

Increase adoption across multiple departments.

**Current Health**

Strong engagement from administrators, but adoption among end users remains lower than expected.

**Risk**

Limited participation in training sessions.

**Opportunity**

Provide role-specific training and share best practice guides to improve adoption.

**Action Plan**

Schedule two additional enablement sessions and review adoption metrics after 30 days.

---

# 📝 Notes

A health check should encourage meaningful conversations rather than simply measure activity.

The objective is to understand where customers are succeeding, where they need support, and how Customer Success teams can continue delivering value throughout the customer journey.
