# 🎯 Customer Success Plan Template

## Overview

A Customer Success Plan helps align customer goals with a structured action plan. It provides a shared understanding of objectives, responsibilities, milestones, and success metrics, helping both customers and Customer Success teams work toward measurable outcomes.

This template can be adapted for new customer onboarding, implementation projects, account planning, or ongoing customer success engagements.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

Use this template to:

- Align customer and business goals
- Define clear success metrics
- Establish milestones and timelines
- Identify potential risks early
- Track progress throughout the customer journey

# 🛠️ How to Customize

This template can be adjusted based on:

- Customer size
- Product complexity
- Implementation timeline
- Team structure
- Business priorities

Use only the sections that add value while maintaining clarity and alignment.

---

# 🌍 Example

**Customer Goal**

Reduce onboarding time for new employees by 30%.

**Success Metric**

Average onboarding time reduced from 10 days to 7 days within three months.

**Milestone**

Complete administrator training by the end of Week 2.

**Risk**

Limited availability of customer stakeholders during implementation.

---

# 📝 Notes

A Customer Success Plan should be reviewed regularly and updated as customer goals evolve. It serves as a collaborative document that keeps both the customer and internal teams aligned throughout the engagement.
